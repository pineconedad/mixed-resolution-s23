\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Feed-forward multi-view reconstruction.}
DUSt3R~\cite{wang2024dust3r} and MASt3R~\cite{leroy2024mast3r} regress pointmaps for pairs of images and align them globally to handle more views. VGGT~\cite{wang2025vggt} processes all views jointly and predicts cameras, depth and points directly. \pithree~\cite{wang2025pi3} removes VGGT's reference view and makes the network equivariant to the order of its inputs. Depth Anything~3~\cite{lin2025da3} predicts depth and rays with a plain transformer and provides the benchmark data we use for ETH3D and HiRoom. These works report accuracy at one input resolution per model. We measure how accuracy changes across resolutions and inside the network.

\paragraph{Resolution and cost in these models.}
Several works reduce the cost of VGGT-style models without retraining them. FastVGGT~\cite{shen2025fastvggt} merges tokens in global attention. AVGGT~\cite{sun2025avggt} finds that VGGT's early global layers form no meaningful cross-view correspondences, replaces them with frame attention, and in the remaining global layers keeps every query while subsampling keys and values on a grid. Good Token Hunting~\cite{goodtoken2026} selects tokens in two stages, first choosing which frames to keep and then removing redundant tokens within them, and reports large speed-ups on scenes with hundreds of images. Other works train new models that separate resolutions: HD-VGGT~\cite{hdvggt2026} adds a high-resolution branch that refines the prediction of a low-resolution VGGT through learned feature upsampling, and DAGE~\cite{ngo2026dage} combines a low-resolution stream for global consistency with a stream that keeps fine detail at native resolution. These methods decide where tokens can be removed or added by designing or training a new procedure, and mostly target scenes with hundreds of frames. Our probes ask the prior question of which stages of an existing model lose accuracy when their resolution is lowered.

\paragraph{Probing pretrained models.}
Probing studies measure what a frozen model represents by testing it under controlled conditions rather than by fine-tuning it. El Banani \etal~\cite{elbanani2024probing} probe the 3D awareness of visual foundation models this way, and Bratuli\'c \etal~\cite{bratulic2025geometric} show that VGGT's global attention performs correspondence matching and encodes epipolar geometry. Vision transformers are also known to be sensitive to the resolution at which they are used: FlexiViT~\cite{beyer2023flexivit} and NaViT~\cite{dehghani2023navit} train models to cope with varying patch sizes and resolutions, and rotary position embeddings~\cite{su2024roformer}, which both of our models use, are commonly rescaled when the input length changes~\cite{chen2023pi,peng2023yarn}. We apply the probing approach to resolution in feed-forward 3D models.
